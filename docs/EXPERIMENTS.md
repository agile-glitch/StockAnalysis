# Experiments: fitted score weights and indicator combinations

Generated by `python -m stockanalysis.validation.experiments`. Rules for changing the app were fixed before running: adopt only if it beats holding on the fresh stocks in both periods and the result is statistically significant.

## 1. Fitting the score's weights

A ridge regression learned the weights from 10,406 stock-months of training data (penalty 0.1, chosen by leave-one-year-out cross-validation). Weights are scaled so their sizes add up to 100, like the app's. Cross-validated ranking skill inside the training years was -0.004 at best: the signals had nothing reliable to learn even before any test data was used.

With nine models and six test sets, one or two such results are expected by chance; it is worth re-checking as more data arrives, not acting on.

## 2. Indicator combinations

14 indicator signals combined into 560 rules (single, both agree, either, two of three). Each trades one shared pot of money across the stocks it holds (max 10% each), the rest in T-bills.

| Rule type | Count | Median training edge | Median unseen-data edge | Share beating holding on unseen data |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| single | 14 | -0.055 | -0.115 | 0.0% |
| AND | 91 | -0.240 | -0.443 | 0.0% |
| OR | 91 | -0.050 | -0.083 | 5.5% |
| 2 of 3 | 364 | -0.045 | -0.180 | 0.0% |

**Best on training data:** 2 of 3: Bollinger breakout (100 days, 2.5 SD) + 55-day breakout, 20-day exit + 252-day momentum, skip 21. Training edge +0.20, unseen data -0.11, fresh stocks -0.10.

**Is the training winner real or luck?** Its better training score came from taking less risk, not from earning more: its daily return minus holding's had a Sharpe ratio of -0.11 a year even in training. Picking the best of 560 tries would produce about +0.75 by luck alone. Deflated Sharpe ratio: 0% probability that it really beats holding (it would look 35% likely if it had been the only rule tried).

Across all combinations, training rank vs unseen-data rank correlation: +0.65. 0.9% of combinations beat holding on unseen data.

**Adopted in the app: no.**

## Caveats

- Large, still-listed US stocks only. Insider and news signals have no history and are not included.
- The combinations' trials are correlated, so the luck correction treats them as more independent tries than they really are, which makes it stricter.
